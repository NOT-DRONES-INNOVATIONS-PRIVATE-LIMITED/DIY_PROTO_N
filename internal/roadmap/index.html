Upcoming

Our next major hardware platform — a new frame designed to carry a 3-axis gimbal and a better imaging stack.

New hardware platform

NOT ORO 3.0

◆ New frame ◆ 3-axis gimbal ◆ Camera / sensor ◆ Battery ◆ Electronics
Components — what's inside the platform
  • New frame — larger airframe engineered for a 3-axis gimbal and heavier imaging payload.
  • 3-axis gimbal — full pitch / roll / yaw stabilization for cinematic-grade footage.
  • Improved camera / sensor — better image sensor with a Sony lens, 4K @ 30 fps.
  • Battery — sized for a 30-minute flight time.
  • Electronics — flight controller, ESCs, radio and power system integrated into the new frame.

What exactly is reused?
  • Airframe & structure — the new frame itself.
  • Power & electronics — battery bay, wiring, flight electronics.
  • Motors, ESCs & propulsion — shared propulsion set.
  • Mounting interfaces — the gimbal/payload mount is the main variable between SKUs.
