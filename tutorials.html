03 · Flashing firmware

Firmware downloads are on the Downloads page.

OTA update procedure

  1. Power on the drone (main board).
  2. Power on the remote — the drone lights switch from blinking to solid, indicating the remote is connected.
  3. On the remote, press S2 (the left switch of the left D-pad). The remote light becomes solid.
  4. Press and hold the top switch of the left D-pad until the drone enters OTA update mode. [pictorial representation to be added]
  5. From your PC, connect to the Wi-Fi network DIY_NOT_xxxxxx broadcast by the drone.
  6. Open http://192.168.4.1/update/ in your browser. You will see a button to upload a .bin file.
  7. Upload the desired firmware .bin.
  8. Once the upload completes, power-cycle the board to boot the new firmware.

Testing builds via the on-board dashboard

If you flashed the barometer, IMU, or motor-testing firmware, open http://192.168.4.1/dashboard/ to view live telemetry, tweak parameters, and watch real-time graphs.

Flashing a different firmware

To switch to another build, reconnect the remote and repeat the two-button sequence (S2, then hold the top switch of the left D-pad) to re-enter OTA update mode, then upload the new .bin.
