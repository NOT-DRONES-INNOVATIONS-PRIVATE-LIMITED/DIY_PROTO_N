02 · Safety

Read these before powering anything on.

  1. Power on and arm the drone only while it is on a flat surface.
  2. Do not touch moving propellers.
  3. Stay at a safe distance of more than 2 m from the drone while flying.
  4. Maintain Visual Line of Sight (VLOS) at all times as the drone pilot.
  5. Flip the drone upside-down to initiate an emergency stop of the motors.
  6. Use the disarm gesture (see How to fly) for an emergency stop mid-flight. There is also a hardware emergency-stop button.
  7. Test motors and electronics without propellers fitted.
  8. Do not connect the battery before the drone is fully assembled and inspected.

06 · How to fly

Power on and pairing

  1. Power on the drone by connecting its battery. The drone light starts flashing — it's waiting for the remote.
  2. Power on the remote by connecting its battery. The remote light also starts flashing.
  3. The drone light becomes solid as soon as it links to the remote. The remote light staying solid means it is transmitting. A still-flashing remote light means standby (not transmitting yet).

Calibration and arming

  1. Press the transmit button on the remote — its light becomes solid and it begins transmitting.
  2. Place the drone on a flat surface.
  3. Calibrate the drone: both lights will flash for about 5 seconds during calibration, then go solid — ready to fly.
  4. Unlock the motors and gradually increase throttle on the left joystick to take off.

Altitude hold

Use S3 to engage altitude hold once at the desired height (release the throttle stick first). Use S1 to disengage. While altitude hold is on the drone maintains height automatically.

Trimming

If the drone drifts on its own without control input, trim with these:

  • Drifting left → right-trim
  • Drifting right → left-trim
  • Drifting forward → back-trim
  • Drifting backwards → forward-trim

Disarm and power off

Long-press the mode button: the remote emits one beep and enters simulator mode (Bluetooth pairing for the simulator app). Press and hold again: two beeps, and the remote returns to drone mode.

07 · Flashing firmware

Firmware downloads are on the Downloads page.

OTA update procedure

  1. Power on the drone (main board).
  2. Power on the remote — the drone lights switch from blinking to solid, indicating the remote is connected.
  3. On the remote, press S2 (the left switch of the left D-pad). The remote light becomes solid.
  4. Press and hold the top switch of the left D-pad until the drone enters OTA update mode. [pictorial representation to be added]
  5. From your PC, connect to the Wi-Fi network DIY_NOT_xxxxxx broadcast by the drone.
  6. Open http://192.168.4.1/update/ in your browser. You will see a button to upload a .bin file.
  7. Upload the desired firmware .bin.
  8. Once the upload completes, power-cycle the board to boot the new firmware.

Testing builds via the on-board dashboard

If you flashed the barometer, IMU, or motor-testing firmware, open http://192.168.4.1/dashboard/ to view live telemetry, tweak parameters, and watch real-time graphs.

Flashing a different firmware

To switch to another build, reconnect the remote and repeat the two-button sequence (S2, then hold the top switch of the left D-pad) to re-enter OTA update mode, then upload the new .bin.

08 · Using the simulator

The simulator runs on Windows 11 and connects to the remote over Bluetooth. Software size is ~100 MB. Download from the Downloads page.

To pair: long-press the mode button on the remote until you hear one beep. The remote enters simulator mode and is now pairable via Bluetooth from your PC. Long-press again to return to drone mode (two beeps).

10 · Troubleshooting & maintenance

Common problems

ProblemPossible causeSolution
Drone not taking offLow battery / motor issueCharge battery; check motors
Drone driftingImproper calibrationRecalibrate sensors
One motor not spinningESC or wiring issue / low batteryCheck connections; fully charge the battery before flying
Poor control rangeSignal interferenceChange location
Sudden shutdownBattery voltage dropUse a fully charged battery

Maintenance

  • Do not operate in rain or dusty environments — dust may jam the motors and moisture may damage the electronics.
  • Keep the drone clean and dust-free.
  • Regularly inspect propellers for damage.
  • Do not overcharge or fully drain the battery.
  • Store the battery in a cool, dry place.
  • Check screws and frame for looseness.
  • Store the drone safely when not in use.

Tips

  • Always fly responsibly and follow local drone regulations.
  • Replace damaged propellers immediately.

12 · Pre-flight checklist

Before every flight, confirm:

  • Battery fully charged and properly connected.
  • Propellers securely tightened — no cracks or damage.
  • No electromagnetic interference near the drone.
  • Drone placed on a flat surface for calibration.
  • Gyroscope calibrated.
  • Flying area is clear — no obstacles or people.
  • Weather is safe — no strong wind or rain.
